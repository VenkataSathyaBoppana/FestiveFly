Festive Flyby

Twice a day, a little character flies across the page you're on: a morning greeting the first time you use Chrome each morning, and an end-of-day sign-off in the evening. During festival seasons the character changes and counts down to the big day: Holi, Raksha Bandhan, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri & Dussehra, Diwali (with crackers!), Halloween and Christmas. The card is always the opposite of your system theme, so it stands out: white in dark mode, black in light mode.

The flyby never blocks clicks, and Esc dismisses it. It can't appear on Chrome's own pages (new tab, settings) or the Web Store. If you're away from Chrome when it's due, it waits and shows up when you come back. Each one appears at most once a day. If "reduce motion" is on in your OS, it fades in and out instead of flying.
