# Festive Flyby Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

Festive Flyby does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

- It stores only your settings (morning/evening toggles, end-of-day time, enabled holidays, speed) and the dates it last showed each greeting, locally in your browser.
- If you grant site access, it uses it solely to draw the animation on the page you're viewing. It never reads, records, or modifies page content, and it doesn't track which sites you visit.
- It makes no network requests and contains no analytics or third-party code.

Uninstalling the extension removes all its stored data.
